Audio credits

Original English dialogue and audio arrangements by the Warborn project. Character dialogue is generated offline; the game plays local recordings.

  • Orc character tone: Tim Rockk — Orc voice, CC0.
  • Elf character tone: Hydroque — Elf From Dragnor, CC0.
  • Dwarf character tone: MaximB — Drunk Dwarf Voice Pack, CC BY-SA 3.0. Dwarf dialogue recordings are neural adaptations with original project lines, mono downmix, level adjustment and endpoint fades. These adapted audio files remain CC BY-SA 3.0. License text.
  • Goblin character tone: artisticdude — Goblins Sound Pack, CC0.
  • Weapons, timber, mining and coins: Kenney — Impact Sounds and RPG Audio, CC0. Layered, resampled, filtered, level adjusted and faded by the project.
  • Cannon, water and masonry: Thimras — Battle at sea, CC0. Trimmed, layered, resampled, filtered, level adjusted and faded by the project.

Offline authoring uses Kokoro (hexgrad/rzvzn, Apache 2.0) and OpenVoice V2 (MyShell, MIT). No speech model is distributed with the game. Human voice and non-dwarf dialogue outputs are offered under CC0 to the extent of the project's rights. Generated audio notice · CC0 text.

Music, UI cues and wildlife sounds are original project compositions. Audition-only contributions by xathien are credited in the repository and are not used in the shipped character dialogue.

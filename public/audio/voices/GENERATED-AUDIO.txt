Project-generated AI audio for warcraft-2-tribute

Original English dialogue and final recordings are generated offline with
Kokoro voice presets and OpenVoice V2 learned character timbres.
Human, Orc, Elf and Goblin output recordings are dedicated to CC0 1.0
Universal to the extent the project holds rights. See CC0-1.0.txt.

Dwarf outputs derived from MaximB Drunk Dwarf Voice Pack are EXCLUDED
from CC0: all63 adaptations remain CC-BY-SA-3.0. Attribution/source/license
and neural adaptation disclosure appear in public/audio/credits.html.
